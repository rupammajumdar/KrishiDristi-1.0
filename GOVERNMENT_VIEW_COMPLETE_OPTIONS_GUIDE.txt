1. OVERVIEW: GOVERNMENT VIEW KYA HAI AUR KISKE LIYE HAI?
================================================================================

Government View (शासकीय डॅशबोर्ड) KrishiDristi AI ka ek macro-level analytics portal hai.
Yeh District Magistrates (DM), District Agricultural Officers (DAO), Taluka Krishi Adhikari aur Disaster Management Departments ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Jab hazaron-lakhon kisan kisi district me fasal ugate hain, to Sarkar ke paas har ek khet me physical survey karne ka time ya manpower nahi hota. 
Government View poore district aur taluka ka automated satellite aggregation karta hai aur ek single screen par dikha deta hai ki:
- District ke kaun se taluka me sukha (drought) pad raha hai.
- Kitne percent khet red zone (severe crop stress) me hain.
- District ke jheelon, talabon (water reservoirs) me kitna paani bacha hai.
- Kahan turant subsidized khaad, beej ya paani ke tankers bhejne ki zaroorat hai.

2. CORE OBJECTIVE & PROBLEM STATEMENT (SOLVED BY GOVT VIEW)
================================================================================

[TRADITIONAL GOVERNMENT CHALLENGES]:
1. Manual Patwari / Girdawari Surveys: Khet-khet jaakar report banane me 3 se 6 mahine lag jaate hain, tab tak fasal barbad ho chuki hoti hai.
2. Subjective & Biased Reports: Paper reports me corruption ya galti ki sambhavna hoti hai.
3. Lack of Real-Time Drought Warning: Jab tak kisan aawaz uthate hain, tab tak ground water aur dam sukh chuke hote hain.

[KRISHIDRISHTI GOVERNMENT VIEW SOLUTION]:
1. 100% Automated Satellite Coverage: Sentinel-2 satellite har 5 din me poore district ka scan karke live roll-up banati hai.
2. Objective Color-Coded Triage: Har khet automatically Green (Healthy), Yellow (Moderate), ya Red (Severe) me categorized hota hai.
3. 3-Click Drill-Down: Collector 1 click me District -> Taluka -> Village -> Individual Plot tak drill down kar sakta hai.
4. One-Click Official Evidence Export: Central relief packages ke liye official satellite audit report generate hoti hai.

Jab aap top bar me 'Government' tab par click karte hain, to screen par yeh options aate hain:

  2. Quick District Switcher Chips: Populated districts par 1-click me switch karne ke buttons:
     * 🌾 Raipur (Chhattisgarh)

     * 🍇 Pune (Maharashtra)
     * 🍊 Nagpur (Maharashtra)

     * 🌱 Bhopal (Madhya Pradesh)
- Action: Kisi bhi district button par click karte hi backend turant us poore district ka aggregated data, taluka breakup aur water depletion calculate karke screen refresh kar deta hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 GOVERNMENT EVIDENCE EXPORT & AUDIT PDF GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [EXPORT EVIDENCE / DOWNLOAD REPORT BUTTON] (📥 Export Official Evidence PDF):
  * Official Government Audit PDF generate karta hai.
  * Report me State/District level summary, taluka-wise NDVI graphs, reservoir depletion percentage aur recommended relief fund allocation tables shamil hote hain.
  * Yeh report State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) ya Ministry of Agriculture ko relief package sanction karne ke liye legal evidence ke roop me bhej sakte hain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 TOP 4 MACRO KPI METRIC CARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yeh 4 cards district ki overall agricultural health ka 5-second snapshot dete hain:

1. [TOTAL MONITORED PLOTS CARD] (एकूण शेत प्लॉट्स):
   - Number: (e.g. `1,240 Plots` registered).
   - Subtext: "100% Satellite Coverage" — confirmation deta hai ki poore district ke har registered plot par Sentinel-2 telemetry chal rahi hai.

2. [SEVERE STRESS PLOTS (RED) CARD] (गंभीर ताण / धोक्यात असलेली शेती):
   - Number: (e.g. `250 Plots` in Red Zone).
   - Subtext: "20.2% of District" — percentage batata hai ki district ka kitna hissa critical moisture collapse ya crop failure ki kagaar par hai.

3. [RESERVOIR WATER SURFACE DEPLETION CARD] (पाणी साठा घट / Lakes Depletion):
   - Number: (e.g. `-21.4% Water Depletion`).
   - Subtext: "vs 5-Year Baseline (14 Lakes/Reservoirs)" — PyTorch U-Net aur NDWI water boundary detection se district ke sabhi 14 pramukh talabon ka surface area compare karta hai. Agar paani 20%+ gir gaya hai, to yeh early drought signal hai.

4. [ACTIVE DROUGHT ALERTS CARD] (सक्रिय दुष्काळ इशारे):
   - Number: (e.g. `18 Active Alerts`).
   - Subtext: "Requires Relief Assessment" — Taluka-level agriculture officers ko inspection ke liye flag karta hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 3-CLICK DRILL-DOWN TABLE (DISTRICT -> TALUKA -> VILLAGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Macro level se micro level tak detail dekhne ke liye.
- Columns:
  1. Taluka Name (e.g. "Jalna Central", "Jalna North", "Badnapur", "Ambad", "Partur").
  2. Total Plots: Us taluka me monitored total plots.
  3. Green (High Vigor): Healthy khet jahan fasal mast chal rahi hai.
  4. Yellow (Moderate Stress): Khet jahan halka paani ka tanav hai (early warning).
  5. Red (Severe Stress): Critical failure plots jahan turant relief zaroori hai.
  6. Avg NDVI: Taluka ka mean vegetation index (e.g. `0.54` healthy vs `0.42` stressed).
  7. Lakes Monitored: Us taluka me kitne water reservoirs monitor ho rahe hain.
  8. Interactive Row Expansion (Chevron Arrow `>`): Row par click karne par taluka select hota hai aur uske villages ka micro-breakdown khulta hai.

4. PRACTICAL GOVERNMENT USE CASES (DISASTER RELIEF, SUBSIDY & FERTILIZERS)
================================================================================

1. Drought Declaration (दुष्काळ जाहीर करणे):
   - Pehle drought declare karne me mahino lagte the. KrishiDristi satellite data se Collector 48 ghante me scientific proof ke saath drought declare kar sakta hai.

2. Targeted Water Tanker Allocation:
   - Jin talukas me "Water Body Depletion" 30%+ dikh rahi hai, wahan drinking water tankers aur irrigation canal releases ko prioritize kiya jaata hai.

3. Fertilizer & Seed Quota Planning:
   - Agar kisi taluka me NDVI aur soil moisture optimal hai, to wahan high-yield fertilizer quota release hota hai; jahan severe stress hai wahan drought-resistant seeds provide kiye jaate hain.

4. Prevention of Relief Fund Leakage:
   - Relief fund sirf unhi blocks aur kisaano ko milta hai jinki satellite report me actually Red/Severe Stress verified hai. Fraud aur fake claims eliminate ho jaate hain.

5. HOW AN AGRICULTURE OFFICER OR COLLECTOR USES THIS VIEW (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

1. Step 1: Officer login karke 'Government' persona select karta hai.
2. Step 2: District dropdown ya chips se apna district (e.g. "Jalna") select karta hai.
3. Step 3: Top 4 KPI cards me Red Plots (Severe Stress) aur Water Depletion % check karta hai.
4. Step 4: Drill-Down table me dekhta hai ki kis taluka me Red count sabse zyada hai (e.g. Jalna North).
5. Step 5: Table row par click karke affected blocks ko identify karta hai.
6. Step 6: [Export Evidence] button daba kar official PDF download karta hai aur State Relief Committee ko forward karta hai.
