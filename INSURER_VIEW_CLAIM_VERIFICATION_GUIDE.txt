1. OVERVIEW: INSURER CLAIM VIEW KYA HAI AUR KISKE LIYE HAI?
================================================================================

Insurer View (विमा कंपनी पडताळणी पोर्टल) KrishiDristi AI ka ek objective, satellite-grounded insurance claim audit platform hai.
Yeh PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) implement karne wali insurance companies (e.g. Agriculture Insurance Company of India - AIC, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, SBI General) aur claim assessment officers ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

Jab koi kisan fasal kharab hone ka bima claim (crop insurance claim) daalta hai, to insurance company ko yeh verify karna hota hai ki:
1. Kya sach me us khet me fasal boyi gayi thi?
2. Kya sach me sukha (drought), flood ya pest attack se fasal ka nuksan hua hai?
3. Nuksan kitne percent hua hai aur actual payout kitna banta hai?

Insurer View third-party objective satellite evidence aur immutable ML audit trail provide karta hai, jisse claim settlement ka time 6 mahine se ghatkar sirf 48 ghante ho jaata hai.

2. THE PMFBY PROBLEM: TRADITIONAL INSURANCE FLAWS VS KRISHIDRISHTI
================================================================================

[TRADITIONAL CROP INSURANCE PAIN POINTS]:
1. Manual Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs): Traditional system me manual CCEs karne me mahino lagte hain aur sample size bohot chhota (1% se kam) hota hai.
2. Huge Claim Delays (6 to 12 Months): Kisan ko jab agle season ke beej/khad ke liye paise chahiye hote hain, tab tak claim ka paisa milta hi nahi.
3. Ghost & Fraudulent Claims: Kuch log bina kheti kiye ya doosre khet ki purani photo upload karke fake claim le lete hain, jisse insurance companies ko karodon ka ghata hota hai.
4. Farmer Dissatisfaction: Asli zarooratmand kisan ko claim reject hone ka dar rehta hai.

[KRISHIDRISHTI INSURER VIEW SOLUTION]:
1. 100% Polygon-Level Satellite Audit: Har khet ke specific GeoJSON polygon par Sentinel-2 multi-spectral scan.
2. Historical Sowing Verification: Sowing date se lekar harvesting date tak 5-day interval ka NDVI curve prove karta hai ki fasal kheti me thi ya banjar zameen thi.
3. Mathematical Loss Estimation: ML Regressor aur 5-year district baseline se exact loss percentage calculate hota hai.
4. Tamper-Proof Audit Trail: Har satellite scene ka exact acquisition timestamp aur pass ID record hota hai.

4. ANTI-FRAUD ENGINE: GHOST CLAIMS & FALSE DAMAGE DETECTION
================================================================================

Insurer View 3 sabse bade insurance scams ko instantly detect kar leta hai:

1. Ghost Sowing Fraud (फसवणूक - पेरणी नसताना क्लेम):
   - Agar kisan ne claim daala ki meri fasal barbad ho gayi, lekin sowing season me NDVI < 0.20 (Barren Land/Soil) raha ho, to system turant "No Active Crop Detected" flag kar deta hai. Fake claim pakda jaata hai!

2. Post-Harvest Fake Claim (कापणीनंतर खोटा क्लेम):
   - Agar kisan ne fasal kaat kar bech di aur fir claim daal diya, to historical NDVI curve me normal maturity peak aur sudden harvesting drop dikhta hai, na ki gradual moisture drought decay.

3. Area Exaggeration Fraud (क्षेत्रफळ वाढवून सांगणे):
   - Kisan ke 7/12 document par 5 acre likha hai par claim 10 acre par daala hai, to map par draw kiya gaya actual polygon area turant mismatch flag karta hai.

5. HOW AN INSURANCE ADJUSTER VERIFIES A CLAIM (STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW)
================================================================================

1. Step 1: Adjuster PMFBY Claim ID ya Plot ID select karta hai.
2. Step 2: Map par khet ki exact boundary aur current NDVI layer check karta hai.
3. Step 3: 'Predicted Loss vs Baseline' card me check karta hai ki loss policy threshold (e.g. >20% loss) cross kar raha hai ya nahi.
4. Step 4: Historical Time-Series table me pichle 4 satellite passes ka NDVI trend verify karta hai ki damage genuine drought/weather se hua hai.
5. Step 5: [View Raw JSON] se raw telemetry verify karta hai.
6. Step 6: [Download Insurer Audit Report] par click karke legal audit PDF download karta hai aur claim ko 48 hours ke andar Instant Approve/Reject kar deta hai.
